I'm a Computer Science MMath student at the [University of
Waterloo](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/), advised by [Prof. Khuzaima
Daudjee](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~kdaudjee/). I am also a member of the [Data
Systems Group](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-systems-group/).

My primary research interests are in database system internals. I am
particularly interested in adapting existing database architectures to modern
hardware.

Previously, I used to be a Software Engineer at
[QCRI](https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/qcri/), where I worked on scaling the
infrastructure of [Rayyan](https://www.rayyan.ai/), a popular online research
platform. Rayyan was eventually commercialized as an independent startup and is
now a [VC-backed company](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rayyanapp) with
250,000+ users.

I graduated from [Carnegie Mellon University in
Qatar](https://www.qatar.cmu.edu/) in 2018 with a BSc in Computer Science, where
I worked with [Prof. Ihab
Younis](https://www.qf.org.qa/media-center/experts/dr-ihab-younis) on building
[tools and pipelines](https://github.com/YounisLab/) for RNA-Seq analysis.
